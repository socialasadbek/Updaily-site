An attacker accessed an employee’s corporate GitHub account, reached 1Password’s source code, inserted malicious code into internal services, and used it to extract customer data. The company began investigating on November 19.

Passwords and user vault contents were not affected. Exposed data included customer names, email addresses, public keys, account and user IDs, plus tokens from some invitation and account recovery links. 1Password has not said how many people were affected.

The main risk is phishing. The tokens are useless on their own, since access also requires SSO or a code sent by email. Those codes are now 10 characters instead of six digits, making them harder to guess.
