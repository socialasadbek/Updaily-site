**Employees in Uzbekistan Can Now Receive Their Salaries on a Bank Card of Their Choice**

Employees in Uzbekistan can now receive their salaries and other payments through a bank card or e-wallet of their choice, regardless of which bank provides services to their employer.

The new option is available through the Central Bank's Funds Distribution Center. Employees can choose where to receive their salaries, bonuses, and other payments.

How does it work?

Employees simply need to provide their chosen bank card or e-wallet details to their employer's accounting department. No separate registration in the system is required.

One important condition is that the bank card or e-wallet must be registered in the employee's own name. Employees do not need to open a new card at the bank selected by their employer. They can use an existing card and change their preferred payment account later if needed.
