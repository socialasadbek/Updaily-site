The banks are:

\- Ipak Yo‘li Bank;
\- ASIA ALLIANCE BANK;
\- Orient Finans Bank;
\- HAYOT BANK.

The licenses allow these banks to carry out Islamic financial operations permitted under Uzbek legislation.

Customers can verify whether a bank holds the relevant license through the official website of the Central Bank of Uzbekistan.

The new licenses expand the number of banks authorized to provide Islamic financial services in the country.
